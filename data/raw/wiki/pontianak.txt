### WIKI: Pontianak | RU: Понтианак | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Pontianaks are a type of creature born from the spirits of women that died during childbirth.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Pontianaks are incredibly powerful creature with some rare and in some cases, unique abilities.
Shapeshifting - Pontianaks are capable shapeshifters. They can change their appearance according to their will.
Invisibility - As a ghost, they are invisible. Except when they want to appear.
Intangibility - As a ghost, they are intangible. However, unusual for ghosts, once they appear and are manifested fully, their body is tangible and corporeal enough to be damaged by iron bullets.
Invulnerability - Pontianaks are invulnerable against most harm, except when pinned in a hole behind the neck using an iron stake to weaken them and put them in sleep …

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Angels - Stronger beings like angels can effortlessly defeat them. Despite being in a weakened state, Castiel was able to hold their influence and defeat them.
Iron - Like all ghosts, iron can affect them. However, they will not simply dissipate like other ghosts. If they are attacked in their corporeal form, iron bullets will leave a trace or wound as it hits the target. Despite that, it won't stop them, unless they are put to sleep.
Iron Stakes - Stabbing an iron stake to the neck hole behind their head will put them to sleep.
Decapitation - Once a Pontianak is put to sleep, they can be killed through decapitation.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Pontianaks are capable shapeshifters, lore said they can shapeshift to beautiful women. In reality, their real form were quite pale and terrifying and possessed black eyes. When fully unleashed, their eyes become fully white and they transform into a monstrous form with a lack of humanoid features. Their faces become very distorted and their womb broken, revealing a monstrous black misshapen fetus with a lamprey-like mouth and sharp-tipped tentacle-like appendages. Their fully unleashed mode only is unleashed when they encounter a very intense situation.

A pontianak is a female vampiric ghost in Malay mythology. The lore of Pontianak is widely spread on South East Asia countries, particularly in Malay-speaking countries like Malaysia and Indonesia. Pontianak is the name that is mostly renowned specifically in Borneo Island, while in other areas they are called Kuntilanak (in the rest of Indonesia) or Matianak (in Malaysia).
