### WIKI: Alpha Dragon | RU: Альфа-дракон | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The Alpha Dragon is the original Dragon from which all other Dragons come.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
In ancient times, the Alpha Dragon was birthed from Eve and was considered, with the other dragons, too strong so Eve slayed a dragon, Hypolyes, and then created the Dragon-Killing Swords. Eve and the knights hunted the dragons and their Alpha. It is unknown if he survived to Eve's wrath or the knights. If he was always alive, it is unknown if he survived to Crowley's hunt to the Alphas.
