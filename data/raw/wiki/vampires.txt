Vampires are a race of blood-drinking supernatural creatures that were once human. They have been encountered by the Winchesters, Gordon Walker and other hunters. However, it has been stated that encountering a vampire is very rare as hunters have driven them to near extinction, although due to their ability to reproduce by transfusion, there only needs to be one for them to repopulate.

Although they prefer human blood, humans aren't the only animals that they can drink from (however one such vampire, Lenore, refers to cow blood as being 'disgusting'). Vampires must keep their victims alive or else their blood becomes toxic to them. However, some vampires will live off of donated blood bags because they don't want to kill a living being. ===Early History===
Vampires were created by Eve and were some of her very first children. The Alpha Vampire was the very first vampire and he appears to have a psychic connection with other vampires. By the year 2006, vampires had been hunted so intensively and become so rare that John Winchester had thought they were extinct. However, they have started to make something of a resurgence, particularly in 2011 after the Alpha Vampire ordered the existing nests to increase their numbers on Eve's orders.
