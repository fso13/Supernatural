Harmonia is the immortal goddess of harmony and concord. She had sex with Ares, and from this were originated the first Amazons. After a war, and their numbers were vastly depleted, the Amazons made a deal with Harmonia to save them from extinction, and she made them more than human.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Immortality - As a goddess, she is naturally an immortal being.
Supernatural Procreation - When she was impregnated by Ares, their children, the Amazons, end up being a new species/type of creature.
Conversion - By making a deal with the Amazons, she transformed them into an even more powerful supernatural creature.
