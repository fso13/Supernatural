The World Turtle, also called a Giant Turtle by Odin is a mythical and controversial being with his existence not being completely confirmed.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Gigantic Size - The World Turtle supposedly is a gigantic mythical turtle from the chinese pantheon that holds the world on its back, supposing that by "the world" Odin and Zao Shen meant the planet Earth, the turtle would have to be bigger than the planet, which has around 12,742 kilometers of diameter, if by "the world" Odin and Zao Shen meant the Universe, the turtle would be at the very least about 93 billion light-years tall/long which is the size of the observable universe (for reference, one light-year is about 9.46 quadrillions kilometers). If it really exists or ever existed, it would be by far the biggest/largest character in the whole Supernatural franchise.
Super Strength - As a side-effect of the Turtle's enormous size, it is able to extend it to exert or apply unnatural amounts of force and pressure onto objects.
Space Breathing - All air stays close to the Earth, if the Turtle were to carry the world in it's back, it wouldn't have access to the air in order to breathe.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Season 5===
In Hammer of the Gods, while a group of pagan gods discuss what to do in relation to The Apocalypse, Odin states calmly in order to tranquilize the other gods that the Apocalypse isn't actually the end of the world and that when the world really ends the Great Serpent Jörmungandr will rise up, while Odin himself believes he is going eaten by a big wolf, Zao Shen then frustrated says "Here we go..." in disbelief, Odin then criticizes Zao Shen's Chinese lore/world view as well, saying that "the whole world's getting carried around on the back of a giant turtle" is ridiculous and that Zao Shen should give him a break, Zao Shen then angrily orders Odin to stop mocking his World Turtle, Odin asks what he is gonna do about it, he says that he is gonna send Odin packing to Valhalla.
