### WIKI: Ghost Ships | RU: Корабли-призраки | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Духи

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
thumb|300px|The ghost ship Espírito Santo.
A Ghost Ship can refer to either a ship that appears as an apparition after it has known to have sunk, or to a ship which continues to sail after its crew mysteriously vanishes. Sam mentions that they are often a Death Omen.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Espírito Santo - This Ghost Ship is a Death Omen. It is described as a three-masted Clipper ship. The name means literally "holy spirit" in Portuguese, after the Holy Ghost of Christianity.
The Flying Dutchman - Sam refers to The Flying Dutchman as an example of a Ghost Ship.
SS Violet - Sam refers to the SS Violet as an example of a Ghost Ship.
Griffon - Sam refers to the Griffon as an example of a Ghost Ship.
