### WIKI: Lore Rakshasas | RU: Лорные ракшасы | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The unnamed Rakshasas were Rakshasas, which were hunted over time.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
The Rakshasas came from Hindu countries but eventually left their country of origin to settle in the Western world, such as the United States. Although they were rare, they were discovered by hunters thanks to their characteristics and eventually killed by them over the years. One of them survived and disguised himself as a blind knife thrower before being hunted and killed.
