Mixcoatl, which means "cloud serpent", is the god of war, the hunt, the stars, and the heavens. He is a member of the Aztec Pantheon and considered a patron god of the Aztecs.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
=== Background ===
Shortly after the "apes" (humans) climbed down the trees and started to pray for the sun and other natural phenomena, God created Mixcoatl and the rest of the deities to be his scapegoats when something went wrong. God also created them because they made for good stories.
