Naomi is a high-tier angel charged with making rebellious angels obedient once more and extracting information from them. Following the slaughter of Raphael's followers at Castiel's hand, Naomi headed one of the many factions of angels vying for leadership of Heaven. She saved Castiel from Purgatory to brainwash him into being her unwitting agent in Season 8. Upon learning of Metatron's plan, she attempted to stop the scribe, only to supposedly be killed by him instead. Years later, Naomi surfaced again revealing that she had survived in secret and is now leading the effort to keep Heaven going.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
thumb|220x220px|Naomi attempt to blast Crowley with holy white light
While her exact rank in the Heavenly Host is unknown, Naomi is an undeniably powerful angel with a high degree of authority over Heaven and its denizens. She was capable of easily smiting Crowley like the Seraph Castiel, making her one of the strongest angels in the series. She possesses all of the basic powers and weaknesses of an angel. 
Angelic Possession - As an angel, Naomi was able to possess a human to walk on Earth. She of course needs the vessel's consent.
Compulsion - Naomi could compel other angels to follow her command. During their conversations, Castiel replied immediately and in full to any question she put to him. She later forced Castiel to murder Samandriel, an order he was unable to refuse. She lost this control over him however after their connection was severed by the angel tablet.

Naomi's drill - She was apparently killed by this device in Heaven. While she survived, the damage it caused required years to pull her thoughts together enough to be "mostly here."
Angel-killing bullets - Naomi fled when confronted by Crowley with a gun loaded with angel blade bullets.

She was fearful of The Shadow's invasion, a fact justified when it was able to possess Naomi against her will and when in search for Jack's soul, it was able to effortlessly defeat Naomi and the other remaining angels.
Insufficient number of angels in Heaven - Like all angels, Naomi is bound to Heaven and serves as a power source for Heaven while in it. With only less than a dozen angels in Heaven, Naomi and the other angels will eventually burn out in an attempt to keep Heaven running.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
From her dialogue, it appears Naomi had been an important player in Heaven for thousands of years. She mentioned wiping Castiel's memory so many times, he forgot events of his past, such as the massacre of firstborns in Egypt over three thousand years ago. It was revealed that she worked directly under the archangels, and was supposed to "debrief" Metatron, following God's departing, but he fled before she was able to.
