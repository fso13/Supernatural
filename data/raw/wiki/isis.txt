Isis was one of the Pagan Deities who met at the Elysian Fields Hotel to try to work out a way to stop the Apocalypse. She was a goddess of Egyptian Pantheon, eventually being slaughtered by Lucifer.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Not much is known of her powers, although as a deity, she is assumed to possess the standard powers seen in other deities.
Super Strength - As a pagan deity Isis wielded superhuman strength.
Invulnerability - As a pagan deity Isis was most likely immune to conventional means of killing.
Immortality - As a pagan deity she could have lived forever, and was thousands of years old, at the time of her death.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Archangels - Lucifer easily killed her. Any archangel could effortlessly annihilate her.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Season 5===
She was at the Elysian Fields Hotel along with nine other deities from different cultures to talk about the Apocalypse and how to stop it. She appeared as a well-dressed blonde woman masquerading as a waitress.

When Lucifer showed up in the hotel, Isis was slaughtered off-screen.
