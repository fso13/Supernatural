The Alpha Shapeshifter was the very first shapeshifter and the one who spawned all the others. They walked the earth, and created more children as humanity grew into civilization. By the time he was captured and killed by Crowley during his search for Purgatory, there were more than an (estimated) thousands of active shapeshifters.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
frame|Unlike almost all of its children, the alpha doesn't shed when shifting.
The Alpha Shapeshifter has all the traits and abilities of normal shapeshifters, though at an tremendously higher level. It also has several unique powers which come with being an alpha. As an Alpha, it is one of the oldest and most powerful monsters in existence, as well as one of the first and most powerful creations of Eve.
Advanced Shapeshifting - It can morph its body to match any human appearance it wants. Unlike the vast majority of its children, it does not shed or peel off its skin while altering its shape. It can change its form instantly into anyone it wishes and also copy clothing and objects, such as jewelry, guns, knives, glasses, hats, gloves, etc. It also has complete access to all of the persons memories, thoughts and skills of one they shapeshifted into. Being one of the Alphas, the Alpha Shapeshifter has very few weaknesses, as it is invulnerable to many regular shapeshifter weaknesses.
Iridium - One of the few things known to be harmful to the Alpha Shapeshifter. Being stabbed with an iridium knife was very painful to him, similarly to how silver affects normal shapeshifters.
Decapitation - Crowley was able to kill the Alpha Shapeshifter through decapitation.
The Colt - Can kill all but five things in existence. The Alphas are vulnerable to the Colt as seen when Sam Winchester used it to kill the Alpha Vampire.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Early life===
It was the very first shapeshifter created by Eve. Its age remains unclear, but as the lore and stories of its children are around thousands of years old, it is likely the alpha has been alive since its mother last walked the earth ten thousand years ago. The Alpha Shapeshifter is one of the very first and most powerful creations of Eve. Lore has stated that Eve created the Alpha Shapeshifter during the beginning of Stone Age, making it around 2.6 million years old. They have taught their children to blend in with humanity, and as technology advanced, so did they. They were able to at some point of time go comfortably underground, until so much has time passed the world deemed them a myth. All but their children, their siblings, as well as beings just as old if not older than them knew for their existence before Crowley began tracking the Alpha's down.
