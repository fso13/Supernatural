Gilgamesh was a ancient Mesopotamian mythological hero, and most likely a historical king of the Sumerian city-state of Uruk.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Background===
Several millennia after God created the lesser deities, the roman goddess of luck, Fortuna, who favored those she deemed "true heroes", encountered Gilgamesh and gave him luck in his reign/adventures.
