On May 14, 1998, hunter Gordon Walker told Jo Harvelle what Succubi were and what they did which caused Jo to write about it in her journal-turned-blog though instead of writing about what she had been told, she insisted that her mother did not need to know and instead wrote about another thing.

[ЛОР]
In lore, succubi are a type of demon that sexually assault men in their sleep and drain them of energy; their male counterparts who do likewise to sleeping women are called incubi. It is said that after Lilith became a demon, she and Lucifer or Sammael had demonic children. These children were the succubi and incubi.
