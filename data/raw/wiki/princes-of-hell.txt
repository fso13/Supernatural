The Princes of Hell were the first generation of demons created by Lucifer himself after the creation of Lilith. Lucifer created them himself before the sinking of Atlantis. In the demonic hierarchy of Hell, they ranked among the highest, eclipsed only by the original demon Lilith. They were originally generals of Lucifer's demonic armies, but have since become separated from Hell, opting instead to hide on Earth. The one known exception to this was Azazel, who remained loyal to Lucifer until his death. After a promise to rule at Lucifer's side, Dagon briefly served him once more as the protector of his son. [ИСТОРИЯ]
===Background===
After creating the first demon, Lilith, Lucifer created the four Princes of Hell as the first generation of demons aside from Lilith. Lucifer created them himself before the oceans drank Atlantis. These demons were Lucifer's rightful successors to the throne of Hell and acted as generals of the demonic forces. They were also created around the same time as Cain when Lucifer passed the Mark over to him, and the Knights of Hell who were also handpicked by Lucifer as an elite fighting force of demons trained by Cain as far superior to regular demons, and were the first of the fallen humans.

[ЛОР]
In demonology, the Princes of Hell are the seven highest-ranking demons, which correspond to the Seven Deadly Sins. The identities of these demons vary sometimes, though Lucifer and Satan often appear as Pride and Wrath.
