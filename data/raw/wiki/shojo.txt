[ОПИСАНИЕ]
A Shōjō is a Japanese alcohol spirit but is different in that it's corporeal and only those who are drunk can perceive them.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Being a Japanese ghost-like creature, Shōjō possess similar abilities as ghosts. However, they are more corporeal. Additionally, they are invisible to everyone, save for those who are drunk.
Invisibility - They are unseen unless the viewer is intoxicated. Their presence can however be captured on film, but they still can't be seen, unless the person is intoxicated. Also like many invisible creatures, they can't be heard, unless, following their weakness, the person is drunk.
Invulnerability - They cannot be killed with conventional means, they can only be killed by a katana that has been blessed by a Shinto priest.
Telekinesis - They are able to move objects without touching it. One such Shōjō, in its hunt it used it to close the door to stop them …

[СЛАБОСТИ]
thumb|225px|A shojo being killed by the sword.
Intoxicated People - A person who is intoxicated can perceive the corporeal figure of a Shōjō, giving away their location and presence.
Katana blessed by a Shinto Priest - A Shinto Blessed Katana can kill a Shōjō (although a Shinto blessing by a normal person will suffice). When stabbed with this blade, it kills the Shōjō and the creature becomes visible for a few moments before its death.
Magic - Using the correct spell, a shōjō can be enslaved to a person's will.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
thumb|225px|A Shojo's depiction in a website.

It can be harnessed to carry out an individual's desires by use of a spell box. The Shōjō can only be seen when a person is drunk though when killed it becomes visible for a moment to all. When hunting the ones it was sent after, a Shojo likes to take its time, approaches its target very slowly, only becoming aggressive and quick when it realizes that others can see it, or when someone or something tries to get in its way.

It also seems to prefer openly attacking its prey when they're intoxicated enough to be able to perceive it, and is fine with simply following them until such time, implying that it likes to play with its victims. Either that, or it absolutely refuses to ambush them when they are unaware …

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Jim McAnn, Randy Baxter and Dale Lampert were the founders the MidWestern Brewery Company, makers of Thighslapper Premium Ale.
When Jim and Randy decide to sell the Brewery to large distributor, overriding Dale's wishes he was distraught. Having acquired a Shōjō trapped in a sake bottle inside a spell box inscribed with "what you took, will be taken from you" from his travels, he sends it to them, before leaving. He then kills himself.
