### WIKI: Morax | RU: Моракс | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Демоны

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Morax was a rank-two demon that served under Crowley.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
At some point Morax stole two infants from Awar, a rank-three demon. Awar took this matter to Crowley, who didn't seem to care.

[ЛОР]
In demonology Morax is a Great Earl and President of Hell, as well as the 21st spirit of Ars Goetia. He is said to rule over thirty-two legions of demons, and teaches astronomy to mortals.
