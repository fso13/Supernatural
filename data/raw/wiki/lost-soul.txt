A Lost Soul is a type of ghost. They're typically restless spirits who are unable to move on for some reason, but are otherwise harmless.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Electromagnetic Interference - The presence of the ghosts at the Meadows' House caused the lights to shatter.
Thermokinesis - The presence of the ghosts at the Meadows' House caused the temperature to drop.
Invisibility - Lost souls are typically invisible.
EVP - Candy was too weak to manifest visibly and could only communicate through EVP.
Telekinesis - As seen with Haskel Crane and the Meadows' House Ghosts, lost souls can develop the ability to move objects with their mind.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Salting and Burning their Bones - Like all ghosts, salting and burning a lost soul's bones typically releases their souls to the afterlife. However, this can occasionally not be enough if they are trapped by a stronger ghost.
Stronger Ghosts - Lost souls have been on multiple occasions trapped, usually in the place of their death, by stronger ghosts such as Whitman Van Ness, Hugo Moriarty and Avery Meadows. In Hugo's case, he was keeping his victims from moving on even after their remains had been salted and burned. Upon his ghost's destruction, Hugo's victims were released to the afterlife.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Lost souls are a type of harmless ghost who appears to mainly be lost and unable to move on, but are neither vengeful or violent.

Some lost souls are the victims of violent crimes who are trapped in the place of their death, particularly if the culprit is a ghost who is powerful enough to trap them. Several of the Van Ness House Ghosts developed what Bobby Singer referred to as "Ghost Alzheimer's" where, over time, the ghosts degraded, developing a condition where they would lose their memory and all sense of self, becoming empty mindless shells.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Season 1===
In Asylum, Sam and Dean investigate the abandoned Roosevelt Asylum which is haunted by the ghosts of the patients and staff who had died there in a riot decades before the bodies of many of whom were never found. Although mistaken for hostile at first, most of the ghosts prove to be harmless lost souls who are actually trying to warn people rather than harm them. One of the ghosts helps the hunters to identify Doctor Sanford Ellicott as the true culprit and locate his remains.
