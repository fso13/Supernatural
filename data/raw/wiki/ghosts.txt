[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Ghosts, also called Spirits, are souls of deceased humans and animals that did not go with their reaper to their respective afterlife and are stuck on Earth. The word Ghost is a common term for a soul that did not move on to an afterlife after they perished. They typically reside in The Veil, but they can occasionally appear in the physical plane. Descriptions of the apparition of ghosts vary widely: the mode of manifestation can range from an invisible presence to translucent or wispy shapes, to realistic, life-like visions.

They are also one of the most recurring "monsters of the week" in the series, appearing in many episodes.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Ghosts possess a variety of powers, with speculation suggesting they may be capable of even more. The following is a brief list of abilities commonly exhibited by ghosts. While not all share the same skills, many have similar capabilities, often retaining skills in activities they excelled at in life. In some cases, a ghost’s powers may be connected to the circumstances of their death. These traits make them among the most formidable entities in Supernatural.

Salt - Ghosts can be repelled by salt and are unable to cross a line made of it. Some hunters employ rock salt to disperse ghosts by blasting them directly. Salt is also effective when a ghost is possessing a human; forcing salt into the mouth of the possessed individual inflicts severe pain on the entity, compelling it to release its hold and freeing the person from its control. Similarly, shooting the possessed individual with rock salt can also break the ghost’s possession.

A ghost is the spirit of a deceased person who, for various reasons, has been unable to move on to the next life or whose rest has been disturbed. Ghosts are neither inherently good nor evil; their behavior reflects who they were life and the circumstances of their death. In the case of particularly malevolent ghosts, it is believed that the darkness within their souls existed their mortal lives and persisted into their next existence. 

Typically, a ghost begins in a state of confusion and disorientation before realizing they are dead. In some instances, the trauma of a sudden death can be so profound that a ghost believes they are still alive, continuing routines from past life, unaware of their true state.

In some cases, their appearance reflects the condition at the time of death, displaying physical signs of how they died. Additionally, ghosts can assume a smoke-like form resembling that of demons, though their vaporous shape is generally a transparent grey rather than black.
