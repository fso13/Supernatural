Pestilence is an ancient, powerful, and fearful member of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, alongside his brothers Famine, War, and Death. He personifies all types sicknesses, viruses, bacteria, parasites, pathogens, and allergen alike.

=== Early History ===
As one of the Four Horsemen, Pestilence was born from the primordial chaos of the universe, representing the inevitable decline of life through disease. Throughout history, he has been responsible for countless plagues, outbreaks, and pandemics.
