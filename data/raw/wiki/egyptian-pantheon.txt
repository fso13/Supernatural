[ИСТОРИЯ]
Osiris was the god of the afterlife, transition, death, life, the dead, resurrection, regeneration, fertility and ruler of the underworld. For some reason, he went to Dearborn, Michigan, and started to put people who felt guilty on trial; if found guilty, they were sentenced to death and Osiris commanded ghosts or specters to carry out the sentence.

It's revealed by Castiel in Season 14 that after God abandoned Heaven, Osiris was considered for the job as the new judge of souls. However, he was passed over in favor of his son, Anubis.
