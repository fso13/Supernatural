### WIKI: Huehuecoyotl | RU: Уэуэкойотль | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Боги

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Huehuecoyotl was an Aztec god of Mischief, music, dance, song and uninhibited male sexuality, and a trickster featured in Supernatural: Coyote's Kiss.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
His weaknesses were never mentioned.
Immortality - As a deity, he was not subject to old age or natural death.
Invulnerability - He could not be harmed by conventional weapons.
Superhuman Strength - He possessed superhuman strength.
Shapeshifting  - Could take on any appearance at will.
Reality Warping - He manipulated a small flame and changed it into a flower, the flower then became a fat horned lizard.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
He was summoned by Xochi for guidance on the Borderwalker menace.

He was the one who guided Xochi that all her problems originated back at home. He also told the hunters about the Alpha Borderwalker and her powers. He tricks Dean by telling him that he should ask for a white copal present in a necklace with the Alpha when in fact that white copal was the very heart of the Alpha. Dean eventually realizes from his own experiences with the vampire cure that Huehuecoyotl actually meant that he needed the Alpha's blood.
