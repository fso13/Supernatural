Erebus was a Greek god. His bracelet and his minions appeared in The Winchesters.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Erebus himself did not make an appearance. However, as a god, he possessed powers and abilities shared by other deities such as immortality and super strength.

 Spell Casting - Erebus had the power to create or enchant a bracelet that had two purposes. To the bearer who is "pure of heart", it revealed monsters. However, if someone with a deep secret wore the bracelet, shadow spirits embedded in the artifact will force the bearer to reveal the secret. If the secret was not revealed, the shadows will consume the bearer.
Super Strength - While not appearing in the episode, it is safe to assume he possesses this power as he is a god.
Immortality - As a god, Erebus is immortal and is able to live forever. He has been around since Ancient Greece.
Invulnerability - Erebus is immune to mortal weapons and cannot be killed by them.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
In ancient history, Erebus created a bracelet that he gave to his followers. The bracelet was devised to uncover the secrets of their enemies. This bracelet came under the possession of Maggie.
