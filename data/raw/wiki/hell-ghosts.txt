[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The Hell Ghosts are the ghosts released by God from Hell when he began the end of the world.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Hell Ghosts are an extremely powerful type of ghost.
Invisibility - As ghosts, they are naturally invisible.
Thermokinesis - Their mere presence also caused the temperature to drop in rooms.
Soul Absorption - Like Whitman Van Ness, Francis Tumblety can absorb the souls of other ghosts, draining them into himself.
Super Strength - The ghosts display superhuman strength capable of allowing them to easily overpower and kill humans.
Teleportation - Many of the ghosts display the ability to teleport.
Ghost Possession - Hell Ghosts are capable of possessing a person's body. They can even possess dead corpses, turning them into a form of zombie.
Immunity - Many of the Hell Ghosts display a high level of immunity to salt and iron, reforming again not long after …

[СЛАБОСТИ]
They possess the regular weaknesses of ghosts, though due to the method of their walking the Earth, they cannot be permanently dispatched in the same way.
Salt - Like all ghosts, they can be kept out and dissipated by salt.
Iron - Like all ghosts, they can be kept out and dissipated by iron. In addition, wearing iron chains keeps them from possessing people and shooting a vessel with iron bullets expels them.
Magic - They have proven to be vulnerable to various types of magic including the Soul Expulsion Spell, the Soul Containment Barrier Spell, the Soul Catcher and Death Is an Infinite Vessel.
Lilith's Crook - As souls from Hell, they are vulnerable to the crook's power which can draw them back into Hell.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Hell Ghosts possess many of the characteristics of regular ghosts, but due to their sheer number, their rise through the Rupture to Hell and the impossibility of locating all of their remains, they cannot be dispatched in a normal manner. While they have many normal ghost powers, they have also shown the unusual and previously unseen ability to possess corpses and in effect reanimate them as a form of zombies. However, these are not true zombies and simply striking them with iron, stabbing them with an angel blade or smiting them ejects the ghost from the corpse.

Their primary weakness is magic which has been able to contain them in various ways. However, working together they are able to overpower and break the barrier created by the Soul Containment …

In Moriah, enraged by the Winchesters' defiance of him, God kills Jack Kline and unleashes the souls of Hell upon the Earth. As the souls being rising from the Rupture to Hell, many tunnel underground into the corpses in the Harlan, Kansas cemetery containing the rupture and rise as zombies to attack the Winchesters and Castiel. Others, such as Mary Worthington, Constance Welch and John Wayne Gacy begin a reign of terror upon innocent people.
