[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Tasha was a werewolf. She was bitten by her sister, Kate, following her being in a car crash to prevent her from dying.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Shapeshifting - As a werewolf, Tasha has the ability to shift between human and wolf form. As a pureblood, she could do this at any time.
Super Strength - As a werewolf, Tasha was very strong, and was capable of overpowering Sam with one hand.
Invunerability - As a werewolf, only silver could kill Tasha.
Super Speed - Tasha was able to move incredibly fast.
Claws - As a werewolf, Tasha had razor sharp claws which she used to kill.
Fangs - As a werewolf, Tasha had razor sharp fangs which she used to eat her victims' hearts.
Regeneration - As a werewolf, Tasha could regenerate wounds that could normally kill humans. She even healed her injuries from the car crash before she became a werewolf.
Infectious Bite - As a werewolf, Tasha could infect others with the curse.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Silver - As a werewolf, Tasha was vulnerable to silver weapons. Her sister Kate killed her with a silver knife.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
Tasha was the younger sister of Kate who was a good girl growing up, but let people walk all over her. She and Kate were close. After being turned into a werewolf, Kate secretly visited her family and saw Tasha and her parents sitting down to dinner, but doesn't go in so she doesn't harm them. Sometime later, Tasha was involved in a bad car accident and wasn't expected to survive.

thumb|left|258px|Tasha in the hospital.
After finding out from Facebook, Kate visited her to say goodbye, but turned her into a werewolf and kidnapped her from the hospital instead as she realized the powers of a werewolf could save Tasha who was surprised by her recovery. Unlike Kate, Tasha embraced being a werewolf and started killing, drawing Sam and Dean's attention.
