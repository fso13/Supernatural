Gavin MacLeod was the son of Crowley, whose destiny was to embark on a ship and die. Abaddon however traveled back in time and took him to the year 2014, where his father saved him from his fate until he chose to return in 2017 after meeting his fiancé Fiona Duncan as a vengeful spirit, restoring the timeline and letting things flow as they did before.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Background===
Gavin was born in Scotland in the late 1600s as the son of Fergus Rodrick MacLeod, the man who would later become the demon Crowley. He and his father hated each other, in life and in death, which stemmed from Fergus being an abusive alcoholic to Gavin. Fergus forbade his son from going to school, resulting in him being illiterate and never told Gavin of his family, such as Fergus' mother.

After his father's death, Gavin buried his father and became captain of a trading ship known as the Star and left Scotland for America in 1723. He died soon after when his ship went down in a storm near Massachusetts. Gavin also had a fiancé named Fiona Duncan whom he loved dearly and wished to join him in his voyage to America.
