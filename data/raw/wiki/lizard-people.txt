Reptilians (also called reptoids, lizard people, reptiloids, saurians, draconians) is a conspiracy theory that many well-known public figures are, in fact, lizard people in disguise. These species, in conspiracy theory, aimed for global domination.

In anti-semitic conspiracy theory, Lizard people are cold-blooded humanoid reptilians who have the power to shape-shift into human form. It was theorized by known anti-Semite David Icke, a so called "new-age philosopher" and one of the most prominent theorists in the lizard people game. Icke has defended claims of anti-Semitism by stating that his theory, which draws on anti-Semitic propaganda (Protocols of the Elders of Zion), is not anti-Semitic because he replaced the word "Jews" with "lizard people".
