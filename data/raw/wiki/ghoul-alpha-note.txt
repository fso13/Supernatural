Alphas are the very first monsters of each species created by Eve.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
As the creators of their specific species, each of them are much more powerful because of literally being the very first of their kind.
Telepathic Link - They possess a telepathic link to all of their children, allowing them to track or communicate with them over enormous distances, literally anywhere on Earth. They can also learn complete knowledge their children possess.
Super Strength - As the eldest, purest, and most endowed of their respective breeds, alphas possess far superior strength than any common member of their species. Alphas possess immense superhuman strength; and are considerably stronger than humans, animals, lesser demons, hellhounds, all other monsters, and literally all members of their species. [ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
The Alphas are the children of Eve and the first of their respective species. As the first and oldest, they are also the most powerful monsters and are regarded as the parent of their respective race, since they were the ones who began populating each monster race once Eve had created them. According to Samuel Campbell, there is a ton of lore about the alphas, but until their emergence when Crowley started hunting for Purgatory, they were thought to be a myth.

Due to their close ties to Eve, the Alphas are aware of Purgatory and the location of Purgatory's gateway, as well as the existence of Leviathans, their ancestors.

===Origins===
The alphas were created by the Mother of All Monsters, whose own origin lore indicates she is a primordial monster, born into Purgatory shortly after the creation of the universe, like the Leviathans.
