### WIKI: Kappa | RU: Каппа | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Kappa are a helpful type of specter that originates from Japan. They protect humans from harm in exchange for food.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Precognition - They can see future events that would cause humans harm, such as a landslide, lightning strike and a car accident. One was even able to see that a man was going to have a fatal heart attack.
Underwater Breathing - They live and thrive underwater but can walk on land and breathe air as well.
Longevity - One of them was well over 100 years old.
Super Strength - Kappa have strength superior to humans. One managed to drag a full-grown man through the woods effortlessly.
Super Speed - Kappa can move at superhuman speed both on land and underwater.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Originally believed to be attacking the people in a small town, Sam and Dean happened to be stuck in. The brothers investigated and thought it was an aquatic demon called a "Yam" but after more investigation it was uncovered that creature was not harming anyone, it was actually saving lives. It had protected a couple from a car accident, saved a man from a fatal heart attack and saved a group of hunters from both a landslide and a lightning strike. It took food from all the people it saved and had a particular fondness for sweets. After Sam tells the towns people that the creature was actually helping them, they all agree to stop hunting it and let it be free.

[ЛОР]
A kappa (河童, river-child)—also known as kawatarō (川太郎, "river-boy"), komahiki (駒引, horse-puller), kawatora (川虎, river-tiger) or suiko (水虎, water-tiger)–is an amphibious yōkai demon or imp found in traditional Japanese folklore. They are typically depicted as green, human-like beings with webbed hands and feet and a turtle-like carapace on their backs.
