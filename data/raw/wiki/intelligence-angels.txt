### WIKI: Intelligence Angels | RU: Ангелы-интеллектуалы | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Ангелы

[ИНФОБОКС]
name: Intelligence Angels
status: Unknown (presumably defunct)

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Intelligence Angels are celestial beings working under Naomi's leadership for intelligence division of Heaven. Their work is to gather information, fix the angels who cause problems, and occasionally, take control of the their minds if needed.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Basic Angel Characteristics - Intelligence Angels possess the same basic powers of other Angels, but presumably stronger than ordinary lower rank ones.
Angelic Biokinesis - Intelligence Angels, such as the likes of Naomi, was able to manipulate the celestial biology of angels and make them go against their nature.
