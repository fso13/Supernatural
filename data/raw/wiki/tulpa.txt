Tulpas created through intense ritual visualizations are known as dubthab. Variation known as dragpoi dubthab is specifically aimed at creating a thought-form with the idea of harming another person. The physical form of tulpas becomes apparent to the senses after the mind can begin to sense its spirit presence. thumb|Tibetan Spirit Sigil.|251x251pxTulpas that are created, no matter the creator's intent, will gradually turn on the creator. Evans-Wentz wrote that enlightened magicians can destroy tulpas as easily as they can create them; also that those masters can incorporate their spiritual being into the body of another person. It's about the willpower.
