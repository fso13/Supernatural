Dagon was a Prince of Hell, one of the first demons created by Lucifer and a retired general of the armies of Hell.

She is one of the three secondary antagonists (alongside Doctor Hess and Arthur Ketch) in Season 12 of Supernatural.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Dagon was a Prince of Hell and thus one of the oldest and most powerful demons to ever exist, displaying many tremendously powerful and in some cases, very rare abilities. It was implied that her powers were enhanced when she pledged herself to Lucifer during the search for Kelly Kline. She was an extraordinarily powerful demon, the only demon powerful enough to easily kill angels with her own power. She was able to effortlessly disintegrate angels to dust with a mere touch, and was powerful enough to easily overpower and kill Seraphim, as she effortlessly overpowered the Seraph Castiel and beat him to a bloody pulp with extreme ease, and could of easily combusted him with her touch of the Winchesters had not intervened. [ИСТОРИЯ]
===Background===
Dagon was the only female among the Princes of Hell, the first generation of demons after Lilith, created by Lucifer himself.

They acted as generals of Hell until Lucifer was imprisoned. Despite being the rightful heirs to Lucifer's throne, Dagon and her brothers Ramiel and Asmodeus opted to retire on earth with only Azazel assuming rule over Hell to continue their father's work. In The British Invasion, it is implied by Lucifer that at some point in the past, she had somehow failed him.

He appears to have been worshipped as a fertility god in Ebla, Assyria, Ugarit and among the Amorites.
