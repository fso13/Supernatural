[ОПИСАНИЕ]
A Woman in White is a type of ghost or spirit, a supernatural creature, that commonly kidnaps men or even children, after suffering from love issues and committing suicide when alive. They have existed for centuries, and have appeared in Hawaii, Mexico, and the United States, as well.

A Woman in White, named Constance Welch, appeared in the first episode of the series, Pilot.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
As a type of Ghost, they posses the common abilities:
Electronic Manipulation - They can manipulate nearby electronics, notably causing lights to flicker.
Invisibility - As spirits, they can become invisible at will.
Shapeshifting - They are able to change from a decayed version of themselves to what they appeared as when living.
Super Strength - They possess superhuman strength.
Super Speed - They are able to move at superhuman speed.
Telekinesis - As spirits, they have the ability to move objects with their mind.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Iron - As any other ghost, they are weak to iron (being hit by a weapon or thing made of iron can make them disappear briefly).
Salt - Which can be used to stop them temporarily and protection circles. However, it's not effective to destroy them.thumb|250px
Ghosts - Bringing the Woman in White to the house where she drowned her children frighten her to confront their spirits and never went 'home'. The children's spirits can overpower her and drag her back to the afterlife.
Burning their bones - Salting and burning the bones of their corpse will permanently defeat a Woman in White. However, at least one Woman in White is known to continue existing even after she was cremated, and, as far as is known, she wasn't attached to any object.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
They are ghosts of deceased beautiful women, who wear white dresses and kidnap people they interact with. When they were alive, they all had children and, when they suffered from a temporal alienation after the men they loved became unfaithful, they kill their children. Then, they commit suicide and, subsequently, they must spend the rest of eternity on Earth looking for their children, crying and suffering, until they find them.

They roam in rivers and roads, and when they find a man who cheats, they kill him and the man is never seen again. If the man they find does not already cheat then they try to make them cheat by seducing them. [ЛОР]
The Woman in White could have been taken from La Llorona (sometimes called the Woman in White), a ghost of a woman crying for her dead children from Mexican folklore, that has extended over Latin America's other folklores.

"La Llorona" ("The Weeping Woman") is a popular legend in Mexico, with many versions extant. The basic story tells that La Llorona was a beautiful woman by the name of Maria who killed her children by drowning them in order to be with the man she loved, but was subsequently rejected by him. (He might have been the children's father who had left her for another woman.) Then, after being rejected by her lover, she killed herself.
