Benjamin was an angel and a member of Ishim's flight.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Benjamin appears to be a regular angel with all the powers of one.
Angelic Possession - Like all angels, Benjamin requires a vessel to walk the Earth and needs their permission. Unlike most angels, Benjamin's vessel was stated to his friend and communicate with him.
Immortality - Unless killed, Benjamin will never age or die. This extends to his vessel as well who remained unaged for over a century.
Astral Projection - As an angel, Benjamin can projected his wings while inside his vessel, onto the surface at will. After gets killed by Lily, Benjamin's wings are burned on the wall of the arcade he was killed in.
Spell Casting - Benjamin was able to broadcast distress signal that was heard by Ishim, Mirabel and Castiel before he was killed.
Invulnerability - As an angel, Benjamin is invulnerable to most forms of harm.
Super Strength - As an angel, Benjamin is stronger than humans, monsters and most demons. …

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Angel Blades - An angel blade could kill Benjamin.
Enochian Magic - Benjamin's powers were ineffective against an Enochian Magic user and he could be overpowered by one without his wings.
