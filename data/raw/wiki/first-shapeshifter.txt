### WIKI: First Shapeshifter | RU: Первый перевёртыш | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The unnamed shapeshifter is a shapeshifter and the first regular shapeshifter created by the Alpha Shapeshifter.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Conversion - He spawned other shapeshifters, which led to the creation of the shapeshifters species. 
 Shapeshifting - He can change his appearance to make himself look like anyone.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
The unnamed man was an ancient human during the stone age. The Alpha Shapeshifter ambushed him at some point and then transformed him into the first shapeshifter, who in turn transformed other humans into the first shapeshifters.
