### WIKI: Eleanor Visyak's Species | RU: Вид Элеонор Висяк | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Eleanor Visyak's Species refers to an unidentified and unnamed race of creature that Dr. Eleanor Visyak was a part of. Being the only one to appear, not much is known about this race.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Possession - Dr. Visyak was able to take over a human's body.
Immortality - Dr. Visyak was more than 900 years old at the time of her death. Her vessel did not age while possessed.
Superhuman Strength - Dr. Visyak was able to easily rip apart a grown man, splattering his blood over a wall. She also seemed confident that she could handle the King of Hell by herself.
Invisibility - Dr. Visyak was able to move unseen after escaping.
Supernatural Endurance - Dr. Visyak was able to endure Crowley's efforts to persuade her to reveal information on Purgatory.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Higher Angels - Dr. Visyak was overpowered and captured by Castiel, a Seraph. Castiel was also able to torture Dr. Visyak to the point she broke, whereas Crowley, the King of Hell, was unable to do so.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
There's been no specification of the date of their creation. However one member of this race has mentioned she is over 900 years old. She crawled through an opening of Purgatory, when H.P. Lovecraft and his friends opened a doorway and took the body of Lovecraft's maid. She then went about killing the members, so the door couldn't be opened again.

Over the years she took the name Dr. Visyak, and lived a life of a normal person, as well as having a relationship with Bobby Singer.

Castiel kidnapped her, and he and Crowley tortured her to force her to reveal information about Purgatory. Dr. Visyak revealed that the spell required the blood of a native of Purgatory, so they killed her to take her blood.

Up to date, she was the only known member of her race to be seen. It is unknown if her species are good by nature, or if Dr. Visyak, being benign and wanting to preserve Earth, was an exception to the rule. …
