[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The Afterlife is where the deceased go after death and where higher entities reside. Most of these dimensions were created by God, except The Empty. It also exists in other universes.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
The Empty was the first afterlife to come into existence before God and his twin sister The Darkness. It serves as the afterlife for deceased angels and demons where they would spend the rest of eternity sleeping and dreaming of their biggest regrets.

Heaven: God created the original Heaven, after locking up the Darkness. The Angels and demons believed The Darkness to be a myth. God, the Archangels and Angels were happy until Lucifer rose up against God. He was banished from Heaven after starting a civil war and refusing to bow to humans and corrupting Lilith. God left due to Gadreel's failure to prevent his first perfect humans from sinning. As the General, Michael had an immovable grip on Heaven for millennia's. After the Team Free Will defeated The Apocalypse, Heaven was sent into a spiral and Castiel the Seraphim took up arms against Raphael. Godstiel dominated the entirety of Heaven, until returning the souls to Purgatory. Over time, Heaven was left in a broken state, splitted factions vying for control. Naomi was a primary player and held significant control over the factions and wanted the Angel Tablet so Crowley couldn't close the gates of Heaven. Metatron enhances himself with the Angel Tablet and ruled unquestionably, expelling all angels out with a spell, causing them to lose their wings. The hierarchy became more distorted and were essentially leaderless for large portions of time. The Darkness attacks Heaven with darkness to get God's attention. After a human dies, they are either sent to Heaven or Hell by a reaper depending on their choices and morals they've had in life. Purgatory is a dimension where deceased monsters go after they've been slain. The Empty is an endless void in which deceased souls of Angels and Demons reside after death.
