[ОПИСАНИЕ]
A Shtriga is an Albanian quasi-vampiric witch that feeds off children's lifeforce and takes on a friendly appearance in society.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Longevity - By feeding off life force, the shtriga can live for an extended period of time; the limits of this are unknown.
Life Force Draining - Shtrigas can have longer life spans by sucking the life force out of children. They can drain the life force of humans of any age, but prefer children. The life force that shtrigas suck out is called "spiritus vitae", which is Latin for "breath of life." And this is not limited to living things, but also encompass those that "once had life", such as trees and plantlife. Due to this, their hand prints cause wood to rot. When they die, the life force they stole will return to all the children still alive.
right|thumb|220px|A shtriga about to feed.
Invulnerability - Shtrigas are very difficult to kill. They cannot be killed with normal weapons like standard bullets and knives.
Shapeshifting - By day, a shtriga can shapeshift into a human so that hunters won't be …

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Consecrated Iron - When feeding, a shtriga is vulnerable to consecrated iron rounds. However, the shtriga must actually be feeding as one was only killed when Dean shot it while it was feeding on Sam. An attempt beforehand when it was interrupted feeding on Michael failed. Although non-fatal, the bullets still seemed to hurt the shtriga, as Hydeker fled when John Winchester shot him and briefly fell to the floor when Sam and Dean fired at the Shtriga years later.
Soulless Individuals - For beings without souls, shtrigas are unable to feed on them.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Sometime prior to the events of the show, Sam and Dean Winchester encountered a shtriga as children. This shtriga had been killing children in Wisconsin every fifteen to twenty years since at least the early 1890s. Their father left Dean in charge of taking care of Sam, while he went to hunt the creature. Dean went out later that night and found the shtriga feeding on Sam. John suddenly arrived in at the last moment and shot the shtriga, causing the creature to retreat and escape. It apparently went into hibernation early as a result as when John returned for it, there was no sign of it.
