Madison McLaughlin (born November 5, 1995 in Baton Rouge, Louisiana) is an American actress best known for her roles in television as Krissy Chambers in the CW series Supernatural and Annabeth 'Annie' Lisbon in the CBS series The Mentalist and as Paige on Teen Wolf. She appeared in the CW series Arrow for multiple episodes as Evelyn Sharp/ Artemis. In 2023, she directed "Leap of Love", a short film for the Easterseals Disability Film Challenge. It won the award for Best Awareness Campaign.
