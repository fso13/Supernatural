[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The Alpha Skinwalker is the very first skinwalker from which all other skinwalkers come. The Alpha Skinwalker has never appeared in the show, but in All Dogs Go To Heaven, it is revealed that he is preparing skinwalkers as sleeper agents in an upcoming war. With Eve dead, it is assumed that these plans have been abandoned. It is unknown if the Alpha survived from the hands of Crowley.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
The Alpha Skinwalker possibly has all the traits and abilities of normal skinwalkers.
Immortality - The Alpha Skinwalker is immortal unlike the skinwalkers and did not die and is well over 10,000 years old when Eve, The Mother of All, had last walked the Earth.
Telepathy - Like all Alphas, it has a telepathic link to its children, thus allowing him to locate them, regardless of where they are. Lucky had told Sam and Dean that the skinwalkers were waiting for the signal from the Alpha, and once it had been given, the skinwalkers numbers go from 30 to 150 in a matter of hours in one night.
Enhanced Super Strength - Being the very first Skinwalker, and the Alpha, he possessed the strength of a skinwalker, but at an immensely higher level, being much stronger than humans and all skinwalkers.
Enhanced Super Senses - As the first of his race, he possesses greatly enhanced senses.
