### WIKI: Edward Trenton's Species | RU: Вид Эдварда Трентона | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Edward Trenton's Species refers to an unidentified and apparently very rare type of monster that Edward Trenton transformed into.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Super Strength - They are strong enough to snatch livers from the body of their victims.
Invulnerability - They cannot be killed by conventional means and weapons, only by silver-coated steel blades.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Silver-coated Steel Blade - The only thing known that can kill them is league between silver and steel. Dean used a silver-coated steel knife.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
There's been no specification of the date of their creation.

They fed on livers by ripping it out of their victims. They could pass on their genetics likely through a bite. It is unknown what they look like besides possessing mostly human features.

In 2003, Dean Winchester hunted and killed the only known member of this species, Edward Trenton. Since then, he commented to not have seen that specific type of monster again.

It is not known if the death of Edward Trenton marked the extinction of the species of if the species is extant or extinct.
