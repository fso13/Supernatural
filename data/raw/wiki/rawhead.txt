name: Rawhead

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
A Rawhead is a type of creature that lurks in basements and preys upon children.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
thumb|200px|A bitemark and tooth left from a Rawhead.
Super Strength - He was able to throw Dean to the ground with one punch.
Super Speed - The Rawhead quickly disappeared after his first attack on the Winchester Brothers.
Invulnerability - This type of creature can only be killed by high-voltage electrocution.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
thumb|200px|A Rawhead is electrocuted.
Electrocution - A Rawhead is only vulnerable to electrocution, so a weapon such as a taser can kill one.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Season 1===
Sam and Dean are seen hunting a Rawhead. They go into a basement, where they find a boy and girl hiding in a cupboard. As they are taking the children to safety, the Rawhead ambushes them.

Sam leaves with the children while Dean fights the Rawhead himself. The Rawhead attacks Dean and throws him onto a wet floor. Despite the fact that they are both in a puddle of water, Dean uses his taser to electrocute the Rawhead, killing it but also severely injuring himself too. Dean is later healed by Roy Le Grange.
