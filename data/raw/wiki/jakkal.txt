[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Jakkals are carrion-eating scavengers and worshiper of Anubis. They appear humanoid, with brownish complexions. Like werewolves, they also possessed fangs and claws. Unlike werewolves, they developed pointed ears and short golden fur sprouted on when turned, similar like a dog. When inactive, their eyes are bright amber colored, a trait that retained even they are in human form. When transformed, their eyes turned bright crimson.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
In ancient times, Jakkals were employed by rich ancient Egyptians to build tombs due, to their skills in magic and trap making. Their kind was known and respected by humans as servants of the great Anubis. Sometimes, in very dire situations, Jakkals were also responsible to summon Anubis to the earth to solve their problem and to help, defeat their enemies. However, as the powers of Anubis are too much to handle, it often causes more destruction than good. Through unknown means, they later seal Anubis in his mummy form, and chose to not call him too often. As time passed, the Jakkals were forgotten by the people.

At some point, they clashed with werewolves. Since then, they had deep feud against werewolves.
