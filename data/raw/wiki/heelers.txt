### WIKI: Heelers | RU: Хилеры | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Heelers, also known as Spring-Heeled Jacks are humanoid beings with enhanced agility. During the 1800s, the British people would refer to it as Springheel Jack due to its ability to jump into the air higher than the average human. In the early 1980s, Ichi, a hunter, tracked one down and would go on to try and find it with the help of Hunter and John Winchester unaware that the one he was after was a shapeshifter in disguise.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Enhanced agility: They could jump higher than the average human which earned them their name as one report claimed an assailant, which was a Heeler, could jump up to fifteen feet.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Although it is unknown when they first came into existence or who created them, Heelers have existed since the 1800s and were described by the British people to murder its victims then leave the crime scene by jumping away. In 1983, Ichi explained that the one that operated in London was in the form of a formally dressed man in a cape who would, much like the legend, target its victims and leap away once they had killed.

Around October of that year, Ichi picked up on reports of a woman being attacked in the woods though despite authorities believing it to be the work of a mountain lion, the hunter realized it was the work of a heeler when it became apparent that the report also mentioned that the attacker escaped by jumping fifteen feet into the air.

Wanting John to work a case, Hunter offered to go and Ichi accepted with Sam and Dean Winchester being left behind at the Harvelle's Roadhouse for their safety. In the woods, the heeler, which was a shapeshifter in disguise, eventually attacked the three before running away to which Ichi ran after in an effort to kill it though he did not survive the encounter and was instead killed by it where upon doing so, the shapeshifter no longer used a heeler as its form but instead the deceased hunter in an effort to trick Hunter and John.

In 2006, Sam mentioned them by the Spring-Heeled Jacks name when he and his brother were sat in a bar.
