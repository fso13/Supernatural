The Mega Coven was an emerging community of witches that were intended to be the successor of the now-crippled Grand Coven. It was founded by Rowena with her newfound power after she mastered certain spells from the Book of the Damned.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
Shortly after the events of Brother's Keeper, Rowena, with her new found power, attempted to recruit witches from the Grand Coven into her own. She tried to persuade them with the Book of the Damned; however, although many grunt-level members of the Grand Coven were interested in the concept, the top members did not believe she possessed it, leading to their deaths by Rowena's hand.

thumb|200px|The Darkness burning the witches from the inside out.In We Happy Few, it was revealed that Clea, a former member of the Grand Coven and Rowena's ally, later joined the "Coven". Clea reminded Rowena that a coven, technically, requires at least three witches. After that, another three members, enlisted by Clea to help in God's plan to defeat The Darkness, joined it. However, all four members were later killed by Amara, making Rowena the sole member again.

It was later revealed that the Mega Coven never became a reality as Rowena quickly abandoned this idea after Lucifer approached her. Subsequently, due to her change in heart and general abandonment of her evil ways, Rowena never revived the idea.
