### WIKI: World Between Worlds | RU: Мир между мирами | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Левиафаны

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The World Between Worlds is a dimension situated between the alternate realities of the Multiverse, it gives access to all universes in existence.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
===Background===
The World Between Worlds was presumably created by God when he was creating the Multiverse. It's a dimension that gives access to any universe in existence, including the Main Universe.
