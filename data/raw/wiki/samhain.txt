[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Samhain, also known as the origin of Halloween and the personification of it, was a powerful and special demon of Hell and was one of the 66 Seals. He could only rise when summoned by two powerful witches through three blood sacrifices over three days, with the last sacrifice day on the final harvest, Halloween. Once risen, Samhain could raise ghosts, zombies, and ghouls, among other creatures. His occult name is Sawin/Sauin.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Samhain is an ancient and incredibly powerful demon who displayed multiple rare abilities as well as abilities unique to himself. That his rise was one of the 66 Seals marks Samhain as a special demon; the only other demon who was a seal was Lilith. His rank in the hierarchy of Hell is unknown. He is one of the oldest and most powerful demons to ever exist, only surpassed by Lilith, Cain, Alastair, Abaddon, and the Princes of Hell in power. 
Summoning - Samhain had the ability to summon creatures without the need for an external power source. Sam stated that the longer Samhain was free, the more dangerous monsters he would summon until all the Monsters and Creatures the Winchesters had ever fought up till that point would come to him.
Necromancy - Samhain could summon ghosts and command them, as shown when he had one attack Dean.
