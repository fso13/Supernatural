### WIKI: Maggie's Shadow Spirit | RU: Теневой дух Мэгги | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Духи

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
This unnamed Shadow Spirit was in Bracelet of Erebus.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
At a unknown point in the past, Erebus created this Shadow Spirit and this Shadow Spirit was imprisoned in the Bracelet of Erebus. The bracelet was later acquired by Maggie Campbell, a member of the Campbell family, who was killed by a vampire in 1971. 

In 1972, after Latika Dar put on the bracelet, this Shadow Spirit was attracted because Lata did not have a pure heart and the Shadow Spirit began to reveal Lata's secret. She took the form of Mary Campbell's deceased cousin, Maggie and warned Lata that she would have to reveal her darkest secret or Lata and Carlos wouldn't live to see the sunrise. Lata finally revealed her darkest secret to Carlos: she stole food from a dinner party Mr. Dar was hosting for his associates and shared it with Sania, their housekeeper who had essentially raised Lata. In search of his daughter, Mr. Dar found the two with the stolen food and turned on Sania and went to beat her. Attempting to defend the woman, Lata hit her father with a lamp. Mr. Dar dragged Lata inside where he told her mother who was disappointed that Lata sided with Sania rather than the family. Realizing that her parents would never look at her the same way again, Lata left for good and never looked back. Accusing Sania of being responsible for his daughter's disobedience, Mr. Dar locked the cleaning lady in her room without any heating. As it was the coldest night of the year, Sania froze to death. After Lata confessed to Carlos, the spirit released them from the bracelet's power.
