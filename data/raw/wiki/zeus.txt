Zeus was the chief pagan deity and king of the Greek Pantheon. He was the God of the sky, lightning, thunder, storms, law, order, justice, destiny, fate and kingship. He was a father to many other of the Olympian deities, including Artemis.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
As a deity, Zeus possessed several abilities common to his kind, however, as the king of the Olympian deities, he was very powerful, more so than most pagan deities. 
Lower Tier Nigh-Omnipotence (Formerly) - When Zeus states, “He is the reason we’re here and not ruling the world,” refers to a former era in which the gods held widespread worship, authority, and influence over humanity, effectively ruling through fear, reverence, and divine power. ===Background===
Long ago in ancient Greece, the Olympian Gods ruled and Zeus was the king of all, but one day Zeus punished humanity by taking away the ability to make fire, leaving the submissive earth in darkness. The Titan Prometheus was not going to tolerate it so he went to Mount Olympus, stole the fire and gave it back to the humans. Zeus, outraged by such insolence, cast a curse on Prometheus that would force him to die in a horrible way and revive for all eternity tied to a rock with an eagle eating his entrails but he managed to escape. After finding out that Prometheus had escaped, Zeus sent his daughter, the Goddess Artemis, to hunt him down but what he did not know was that Artemis was secretly in love with Prometheus and each time she found him, she turned a blind eye to give him the necessary time to escape her father's wrath.
