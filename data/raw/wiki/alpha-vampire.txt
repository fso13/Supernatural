The Alpha Vampire was the very first vampire and progenitor of all other vampires. He was one of the Alpha monsters captured and interrogated by Crowley and Castiel in their efforts to locate Purgatory. He escaped Crowley's prison and set up a new nest in North Dakota, and then Montana. He was later tracked down for his blood for the weapon to defeat the Leviathans. Ultimately recognizing a common enemy, he gave the Winchesters what they asked for without a fight. During an attack on the British Men of Letters, the Alpha Vampire was killed by Sam Winchester with the Colt.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
The Alpha Vampire has all the traits and abilities of normal vampires, although they are tremendously enhanced due to his highly advanced age and status as the Alpha. As an Alpha, he was one of the oldest and most powerful monsters to ever exist. He noted that he is the very first Alpha and the very first monster Eve ever created. He is unquestionably one of the oldest monsters to ever exist, only surpassed by his mother/creator Eve and the Leviathans. He is immeasurably stronger than the oldest of his children, virtually all other monsters, and all average, black-eyed demons. 
Immortality - The Alpha Vampire will live forever, provided he has a steady supply of blood and is not decapitated. He is completely immune to age, diseases, illnesses, infections, viruses, poisons, toxins, and natural death. He is extremely ancient and is very old, even by monster standards. ===Early life===
It is unknown exactly how old he is, but he mentions he was "the thing in the dark" when humans first created fire, as well has having lived through "centuries of fire, ice, rain and continental divide", implying he's hundreds of thousands to millions of years old. When asked, he admitted to knowing about the leviathans, correlating to his age. He was created by Eve, like all the other alpha monsters, and is implied to have been the very first monster she created.

He created an entire race of vampires across the world that soon populated into a new civilization. He kept close tabs on his "children" while continuing to populate the world with Vampires for many years.
