Moloch, also known as Milcom, was the god of sacrifice whom the Bishop Family used for over a hundred years to gain riches.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Moloch was a very ancient and incredibly powerful god, being around for at least over 3 millennia. However having lost all his worship and being kept in a constant state of starvation for a century, he was left even more weakened than other gods.
Reality Warping - In exchange for a sacrifice of blood, Moloch was able to make the Bishop Family rich and prosperous.
Immortality - As a pagan deity, Moloch had an infinite lifespan and could of lived forever. He was alive since at least since Ancient Canaan making him well over 3000 years old.
Invulnerability - Moloch wasn't subject to disease or any other natural causes of harm or death. Being stabbed with a meat hook only angered him.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Around 1898, Moloch was captured by a member of the Bishop Family who then starved Moloch until he agreed to grant the family riches in exchange for a sacrifice of blood every year. Each year from 1898 until 1997 the family fed Moloch who kept them rich. However, in 1997, Barrett Bishop, the head of the family, died and his son, Barrett Bishop Jr., refused to continue on with his work. Instead, Barrett locked Moloch away in the hope that he would starve to death.

In 2017, Moloch was rediscovered by Barrett's half-brother Pete Garfinkle and promised to make him rich if Pete would feed him. Pete kidnapped Jarrod Hayes and Daryn Boston and sacrificed them to Moloch. As Moloch had been starved for 20 years, he needed more blood than normal to get back up to full power and grant Pete his promised riches.
