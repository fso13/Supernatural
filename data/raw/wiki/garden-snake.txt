 The cover art on the Season 6 DVD collection box set/cover art on the Season 6 Blu-Ray Disc collection box set shows a snake coiled around Sam's forearm, to imply a planted spiritual evil seed and is symbolism for cunning, stealth, etc. In biblical lore, Satan shapeshifts into (and somtimes possesses, varies on interpretation) in The Book Of Genesis to tempt Adam and Eve to sin in the Garden of Eden. In the television show, the term 'Serpent' is used to refer to Lucifer, in episodes such as The End and Holy Terror. During the first half of the season, Dean speculated that Lucifer may be influencing Sam and returned from Hell in possession of his body, which was disproven by Castiel.
