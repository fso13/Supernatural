[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Fortuna is a Roman goddess of luck who often helped those she deems true heroes. In the 21st century, she operates a pool hall where individuals can wager their luck for the opportunity to win more with a special coin. Fortuna gradually drains the luck from her patrons until it depletes completely, ultimately leading to their demise.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Like all pagan gods, Fortuna was created by God himself to take the blame for when things went wrong and because the pagan gods made good stories. Unlike most of her kind, Fortuna still remembers the truth about her creation. Over the next several millennia, Fortuna established herself as a goddess of luck who favors those she considers true heroes. Amongst those Fortuna deemed worthy and helped were the legendary heroes Hercules, Cú Chulainn and Gilgamesh. However, as time went on, she began to believe that true heroes no longer existed.

In the 21st century, Fortuna opened a pool hall in Alaska where people bet their luck against each other where Fortuna poses as a customer named Moira whose sister is in a coma and uses her son Pax as manager. At the pool hall, Fortuna gives luck to those who win and takes luck from those who don't. The losers are often trapped in the hall, trying desperately to regain the lost luck caused by their winning streaks eventually turning sour.
