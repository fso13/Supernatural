The unnamed cupid is an angel particularly known for causing John and Mary Winchester to fall in love on Heaven's behest in order to conceive Sam and Dean Winchester to act as Michael and Lucifer's vessels during the Apocalypse.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Angelic Possession - The Cupid can't materialize a full body on its own so it required a vessel to make itself known. 
Pathokinesis - The Cupid could make people fall in love, but one of his fellow cupids said he had an especially hard time with John and Mary. 
Invisibility - The Cupid can make himself invisible to the nake eye; cherubim have a thing for doing their work in secret. 
Immortality - As an angel he will never age or die unless killed
