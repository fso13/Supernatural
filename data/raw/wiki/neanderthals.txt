### WIKI: Neanderthals | RU: Неандертальцы | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The Neanderthals were a human species distinct from Homo Sapiens but belonging to the same genus, Homo.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
The Neanderthals evolved from previous transition forms in nature. The males hunted animals in the wild and the females gathered it to make food similar to H. sapiens. The Great Spirit Messenger read their stories in the cave paintings on the walls.
