Il Soarta was a vampire clan.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
Il Soarta was a vampire clan who worshiped the Ursitoare, the Romanian equivalent of the three Fates of Greek mythology. The Il Soarta believed that if they sacrificed enough blood to the Ursitoare, they would be rewarded with a stone carved from their ribs. This stone was known as the Ursitoare Gem, with the stone, vampires could know the fate of the living - which humans to turn to, who to kill, and who should be replaced within the clan to ensure their success and longevity. The use of the Ursitoare Gem became a sacred practice among the Il Soarta, but when their leader saw that he was destined to die, he chose to attempt to save himself rather than the clan. In doing so, he died as expected and the Ursitoare jewel was lost by the Il Soarta. 

In 1972, still searching for the Ursitoare Gem, the Il Soarta attacked a convoy carrying a map leading to the Men of Letters Clubhouse, the last owners of the Ursitoare Gem. Led by vampire Nikolas, they attacked Diana, an expert on the history of the Ursitoare Gem, as well as the Monster Club, which had come to seek information at Diana's library. The latter was killed, and John had a vision of the future in which he saw himself dying at the hands of Nikolas. Later, Nikolas and the other vampires conducted a raid on the clubhouse.
