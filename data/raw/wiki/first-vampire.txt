### WIKI: First Vampire | RU: Первый вампир | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The unnamed vampire was an ancient human turned into the first vampire ever made by the Alpha Vampire.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Conversion - He spawned other vampires, which led to the creation of the vampires species.
Immortality - If he is still alive, he would be 2 millions years old.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
During stone age, the man was visited upon by Prometheus and given fire. He and his tribe huddled around the fire to stay warm and the Alpha Vampire took him into the shadows and fed on him, transforming him into the first regular vampire.
