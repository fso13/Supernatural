### WIKI: Ghost Banshee | RU: Банши-призрак | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Духи

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
A Ghost Banshee is a type of ghost.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Invisibility - Roxy Carmichael is usually invisible unless she is attempting to draw attention to herself.
Telekinesis - Roxy is able to fling objects with her mind.
Terrakinesis - While cackling, Roxy is able to shake Manfred Afiri's house.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Salt - As seen with Roxy Carmichael, like all ghosts they can be dissipated with salt. However, they are more vulnerable to salt than most ghosts and take longer to recover from a shotgun blast of it.
Salting and Burning of Remains - Like all other ghosts, they can be put to rest by salting and burning their remains.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Ghost Banshees appear to mainly manifest through house-shaking laughter and demands to "love me." They're the ghost of someone who has been murdered by a spurned lover and then refused to move on when their reaper came.

Banshees could be given peace by solving their murder and getting them justice. However, it wouldn't put them to rest which required either their reaper returning or their remains being salted and burned.
