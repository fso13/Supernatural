[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Cherubs, also called Cupids by humans, are a type of angel that seem to be at the very bottom of Heaven's hierarchy. They are tasked with pairing important bloodlines together by means, as it is their chore.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Being a type of low-tier angel, they possess a significant amount of power, but nothing compared to the power of higher angels. Cupids are naturally invisible, they flit with their wings and their true form glows sunnily. They can manipulate the fosterence of love, and are often docilistic and pacifistic, but do wield angel blades. They are happy-go-lucky, but easily saddened.

The Cupids' bow is one of the ingredients for the spell to banish all angels from Heaven. They seemed to possess less grace than any other type of angel, as the stream of grace Lucifer absorbed from one was small.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
In lore, their true form is made up of a human stature, the face of an eagle, ox, lion, and a human, feet of a calf, and four wings. Whether they're like this in Supernatural or not is unknown. Interestingly, Zachariah has stated his true form appears like this, though he was never stated to be a cherub. Cherubim in angelology are the warriors, particularly the ones who guarded the Garden of Eden. In lore, they are actually the second-highest class of angel, not the lowest. Their conflation with Cupid comes from putti, pudgy winged babies, being used to represent them during the Renaissance when they had also been used to represent Cupid.
