[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Heaven ghosts are a type of ghost formed when a soul existing in Heaven is able to manifest on Earth. They are unique, having been created after Jack Kline became the new God and allowed souls to traverse the Multiverse under the condition that they not interfere in any of the worlds that they encountered.

Unlike typical ghosts, Heaven ghosts are able to manifest in physical form in the worlds that they visit. However, although able to take on corporeal form, they remain dead. As such, Heaven ghosts retain at least a few typical ghost powers such as teleportation and invulnerability to things that would harm a living person.
