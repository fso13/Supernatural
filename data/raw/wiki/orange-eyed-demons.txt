### WIKI: Orange-eyed Demons | RU: Оранжевоглазые демоны | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Демоны

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Orange-eyed Demons are a rare type of demon mentioned in Bobby Singer's Guide to Hunting, Bobby himself never encountered one, but heard stories about it.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Possession - Bobby mentioned that these demons like to possess newly married women and kill their husbands.
