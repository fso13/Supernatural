The Loch Ness Monster was first mentioned in a case involving a vengeful spirit in Lake Manitoc, Wisconsin. It was initially suspected as the culprit by Sam and Dean, but the former dismissed it due to a lack of eyewitnesses and sightings of a monster, unlike that of in Loch Ness and Lake Champlain.

Bobby reaffirmed their existence years after when he was asked about dragons by Sam and Dean. Little else is known about these creatures.

[ЛОР]
The Loch Ness Monster is a cryptid in cryptozoology and Scottish folklore that is said to inhabit Loch Ness in the Scottish Highlands. It is often described as large, long-necked, and with one or more humps protruding from the water. The scientific community regards the Loch Ness Monster as a phenomenon without biological basis, explaining sightings as hoaxes, wishful thinking, and the misidentification of mundane objects.
