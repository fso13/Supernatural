[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Acheri is a unique type of demon that can materialize their own form without the need of the use of a vessel, generally, if not always, taking the form of little girls.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Body Materialization - Unlike all other demons in the show besides Daeva, the Acheri is the only type of demon that can take a physical form without possessing a body.
Invisibility - Acheri can become invisible so they can play tricks on humans before killing them.
Shapeshifting - They take the shape of little girls so that people trust them. The demon can reveal its true form when it is about to kill its prey.
Super Strength - Like all demons, Acheri are physically stronger than humans and can rip them apart.
Super Speed - Acheri are much faster than humans. One was able to write "I will not kill" all over a blackboard in a matter of seconds and …

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Salt - They are not able to cross or touch a line of salt.
Iron - Iron can dissolve their physical form.
Special Children - They could be controlled by Ava Wilson.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
thumb|Human Form|167x167px
Unlike most demons, Acheri can take the form of a human without the need to take over a vessel. They hide the second form with features that any typical human would call monstrous.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
=== Season 2 ===
In All Hell Breaks Loose - Part 1, Ava Wilson summoned and controlled an Acheri, with her powers, using it to kill both Lily and Andrew.

[ЛОР]
In Indian mythology, an Acheri is the ghost, or spirit, of a little girl who comes down from mountains and hilltops at night to bring sickness to humans, particularly children. The only defense against an Acheri was thought to be a red ribbon tied around one's neck.
