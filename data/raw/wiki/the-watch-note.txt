### WIKI: The Watch | RU: Дозор | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Левиафаны

[ИНФОБОКС]
name: The Watch
season: 15
powers: Chronokinesis

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
The Watch or God's Watch is a time watch that God used to manipulate Sam.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
The Watch can show its user their future. By setting it to a particular date, the user can see what will happen to them on that date. However, it can't show what happens after the user's future death.
