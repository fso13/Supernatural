This unnamed Loup-Garou was used by the Men of Letters as a watchdog in order to protect the Monster Box.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Loup-Garou appear to be a fairly strong type of monster.
Deadly Claws - The Loup-Garou have deadly claws.
Super Strength - Loup-Garou are stronger than humans.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Silver - Loup-Garou can be harmed by silver, although it can't kill them.
Decapitation - Loup-Garou can only be killed by decapitation.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
At some point, the Men of Letters leave this Loup-Garou as a watchdog to protect the Monster Box. He is captured and lobotomized for the task by Jack Wilcox, the Men of Letters Master Physician who lobotomizes a number of monsters to act as watchdogs for the Men of Letters' most secure locations.

In 1972, the monster attacks John Winchester and Mary Campbell after they retrieve the Monster Box. Mary explains to John what the monster is, but their only silver blade is on the surface in Carlos Cervantez's van. Hiding, John cuts the shrapnel from Hank Murphy's silver cross out of his arm as a weapon, but Mary doesn't think that it will be enough to stop the watchdog. However, John knows that the shrapnel will piss it off and buy Mary enough time to escape. Leaving Mary to escape, John runs off to face the monster.
