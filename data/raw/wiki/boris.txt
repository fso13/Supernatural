Boris was a vampire of over 600 years of age who led a vampire nest. On the orders of the Alpha Vampire, he recruited and turned large numbers of people.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Being over 600 years old, Boris was a powerful vampire, and possessed abilities common to his kind, but due his advanced age, his supernatural abilities was at an considerably higher level.
 Super Strength - Boris possessed considerable superhuman strength; he could effortlessly overpower his victims, and could easily overpower Dean, and casually send him flying over 30 feet through the air with just a backhand to the face, and restrain and beat him to a bloody pulp without any effort. Being a vampire over 600 years old, he was much stronger than humans and younger vampires. He was the strongest vampire in his nest.
 Super Speed - Boris possessed superhuman speed; he could move much faster than any human; he could suddenly appear and disappear from sight in a blink of an eye, and was able to quickly disarm and overpower Dean, just moments after hearing a blood drop from the syringe Dean was holding.
