### WIKI: Alpha Wendigo | RU: Альфа-вендиго | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Монстры

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Despite whether or not Wendigos have an Alpha on Supernatural, in real Wendigo lore, Wendigos do have an Alpha called the Master Wendigo. According to Native Cree lore, The Master/Alpha Wendigo, is 30 feet tall, covered in white fur, and has antlers and mouth full of fangs and gigantic claws. The Alpha Wendigo is claimed to live in Northern Canada and has the power to control the weather to make snow storms or ice storms, can bend lesser wendigos to its will, and can infect any person into a Wendigo without having the person commit cannibalism. It is the very first and strongest Wendigo, tremendously stronger than all Wendigos.

Its existence has been confirmed by Bobby Singer
