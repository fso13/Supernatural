Lenore was a vampire, and leader of a nest that fed on cattle blood rather than human blood.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
Lenore possessed the standard powers and abilities of a vampire.
Super Strength - As a vampire, Lenore was stronger than humans, and could contend with other monsters.
Super Speed - As a vampire, Lenore was much faster than humans; allowing her to suddenly appear and disappear, move and run faster than the human eye could follow, and very quickly hunt down prey.
Super Senses - As a vampire, Lenore had heightened, predatory senses of sight, smell, taste, and hearing. Lenore could hear a humans heartbeat from a vast distance, see perfectly in darkness, and catch the scent of blood in the air; the smell would cause her fangs to extend.
Fangs - Lenore possessed superhumanly strong and razor-sharp fangs, that would extend and retract over her teeth.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
===Season 2===
Lenore was the leader of a group of vampires who had sworn off consuming human blood; instead choosing to sustain themselves on the blood of cows. She explained to Sam that whilst she found cow blood disgusting, it was worth being able to live in peace and avoid the attention from hunters.

Despite her efforts, though, Lenore and her nest were hunted by an obsessed vampire hunter named Gordon Walker, whom Sam and Dean had just met whilst investigating a case. Lenore’s group kidnapped Sam from his motel room and brought him to their nest. There, Lenore asked Sam to help the vampires escape and stop Walker from killing them.
