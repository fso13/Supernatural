### WIKI: Soul | RU: Душа | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Духи

[ИНФОБОКС]
name: Souls

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Souls are the spiritual life essence of a human or monster, which resides within a physical body while they are still alive. After their physical body dies, or is destroyed, their soul continues to exist, and typically moves on to an afterlife. After death, humans can refuse to move to an afterlife, and remain on Earth as a ghost. If they go to Hell, they will end up being tortured into a demon. Monster Souls go to Purgatory. And when a demon gets killed, the soul goes to The Empty.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
Souls are inherently magical and can be harnessed by various beings to cast spells or enhance their power. They are considered both vulnerable and transient, yet also resilient, as they can be harmed but not destroyed. Death once asserted that even he could not shatter a soul; however, it was later demonstrated that the Inner Key Of Oz could achieve this, proving it possible. This may suggest that Death meant such an act cannot occur without consequences, as splitting a soul also divides a person’s nature, creating distinct good and evil counterparts in the cases observed. Notably, the separated halves maintained a physical connection, causing them to share any damage inflicted. Additionally, Sam Winchester noted that Shtriga favor preying on children due to their stronger life forces compared to adults.

It was once believed that burning a ghost’s bones would completely destroy its soul, but this has been proven false. [ИСТОРИЯ]
When God created humans, he gave them a spiritual essence or soul, so that after their physical bodies perished, they could move on to either be rewarded or punished, depending on the life they chose to lead. Eve, the Mother of All, created monsters whose souls function similarly, but are destined instead to Purgatory.

thumb|250px|The boys attempt to rise Bobby to Heaven.
In the Natural Order, human souls are transported to the afterlife by Death's servants, Reapers; however, the reaper Tessa refused to tell Dean on more than one occasion where she takes them. Known destinations of souls are Heaven, Hell, The Empty and Purgatory. However, when Metatron closed Heaven the human souls were stuck in the Veil, waiting for its reopening. Souls have since been shown being able to move out of the Veil once more, how this was accomplished is uncertain.

According to God, creating souls had made him feel "nauseous".
