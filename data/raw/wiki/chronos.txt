As a Pagan deity, Chronos was very powerful, even for deities, as he was much stronger than most deities; as such, he possessed several incredible and rare powers.
Invincibility (formerly) - During the height of the Greek pantheon, Chronos was near indomitable and possessed infinite control over time. 
Immortality - As a deity, Chronos didn't age and could live forever.
Super Strength - Chronos could casually overpower full-grown men; he threw Sam across a room with one hand, effortlessly disarmed Dean and slowly choke him to death, and overpowered Ness and threw him through a garage wall.
Super Stamina - He never tires, regardless of what he had been through.
Invulnerability - Few things could damage him, as he barely reacted to punches in the face, and didn't react at all to being shot twice in the back.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Like most of his kind, Chronos was a deity created by God. It is unclear whether he was a first-generation deity or the son of other deities.

In Antiquity, people summoned Chronos to compel him to tell them their futures. When he was worshiped, he gained his power from taking time from his worshipers. No longer worshiped over the centuries, Chronos was forced to feed after a couple decades on three humans to stop wandering through time. He eventually met a human woman, Lila Taylor who was intended to be one of his victims. However, he instead fell in love with her. Although he didn't tell Lila who he really was, he was forced to kill in order to return to her. His powers sent him into the future so he needed power to return to her. He made a living in 1944 using his foresight to bet on horse races, buying himself a luxurious household until his bookie, Lester Young, became scared of him and cut him off.
