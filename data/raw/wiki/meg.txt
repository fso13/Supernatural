You may be searching for Meg Masters (Demon), the demon possessing human Meg in most of the human's appearances.

Megan "Meg" Masters was the first host for an unnamed demon in October of 2005. Prior to her death, Meg was a college student who had a younger sister that looked up to her. After her death and exorcism, the demon would still use Meg's name upon taking a new vessel.

[ПРЕДЫСТОРИЯ]
Meg was born and raised in Andover, Massachusetts and the first born daughter to her parents. Sometime after her birth, she would gain a younger sister that adored her.

During one night in October, 2005, she attempted to walk home only to be jumped by smoke which was a demon in need of a vessel. During her possession, the demon forced her to have her hair cut short and dress in clothing different than she would wear. Sometime during the possession, she was forced to kill her own sister which she later admitted to Sam and Dean Winchester though referred to the incident as the sister getting lost though when her spirit was summoned to confront Dean, she confessed her death drove her sister to kill herself.

When she made her way to Singer Salvage Yard in November, 2006 to find the Winchesters, Bobby Singer explained that while they were talking to a demon, the girl was just a vessel before further stating that even if they were to exorcise the demon out, Meg would die due to falling from a seven story building and that the demon was inhabiting a corpse.
