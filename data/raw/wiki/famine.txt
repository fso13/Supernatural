Famine, also known as the Black Horseman, is a powerful entity and one of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, along with Death, War, and Pestilence. He manifest physically on Earth by taking human form. He personifies the concept of famine and hunger.

[СИЛЫ И СПОСОБНОСТИ]
As a Horseman, Famine possesses great power, but due to agricultural improvements, he has been significantly weakened. Even weakened, he can easily telekinetically overpower demons and affected Castiel's vessel while he was cut off from Heaven and made him gorge on hamburger meat. Lucifer wanted to aid him up back to full power where his powers would increase exponentially. Like his brothers, he requires his ring to help him focus his powers.
Hunger Dominance - With his ring acting as a conduit and a means to focus his abilities, Famine possesses absolute dominion over the concept of hunger itself.

[ЛОР]
thumb|255x255px|Horseman
In apocalypology/studies of the end times, famine is understood to be given power of hunger over a fourth of the earth. It is neither human nor spirit, but the consequence of sin in the world. It will ride across the lands on a black horse called Fear with scales in its hands. It will speak the words to travelers, "A measure of wheat for a penny, and three measures of barley for a penny; and see thou hurt not the oil and the wine". Under divine law, it is not to touch oil or wine but there will be no rain for months at a time and the soil will go bad.
