### WIKI: Hell's Torturers | RU: Палачи Ада | КАТЕГОРИЯ: Демоны

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Hell's Torturers is a position in Hell designated to a demon specialized in torture, and their leader is called The Grand Torturer. When John Winchester made a deal for Dean's life and died, he went to Hell and there was tortured by Alastair, the Grand Torturer at the time. He offered to reduce John's suffering if he tortured others damned souls, this didn't work for a century. When the same was done to John's son Dean, he refused it for 30 years before he finally couldn't take the suffering anymore and accepted, unknowingly opening the first seal.
