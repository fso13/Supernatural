actor: Various

[ОПИСАНИЕ]
Vengeful Spirits are a dangerous type of ghost that is among the most recurring beings on the series.

[СЛАБОСТИ]
Salt - Feeding salt in the mouth of the person a vengeful spirit is possessing will cause the ghost great pain the point of forcing them out, freeing the human from the ghost's control. Shooting the person with rock salt will also at least harm the ghost.
Salt Water - It can be used to depower Vengeful Spirits when possessing someone.
Salt Water Soaked Ropes - Sam and Dean Winchester used a rope soaked in salt water to restrain, depower and prevent the vengeful spirit of Dirk MacGregor, Jr. from leaving a physical body while he was possessing a bus driver, even though the two seemed pretty confident that the rope would restrain Dirk for good. He was eventually able to free himself when enraged.
Salting and Burning their Mortal Remains - Dirk MacGregor, Jr. was cremated upon death but was able to attach himself to a bit of his hair his dad kept in a bible. After Dean burned the hair, Dirk was put to rest.

[ХАРАКТЕРИСТИКИ]
They are ghosts who have had some great wrong done to them in their lives (normally what killed them). This causes them to remain after death to try and avenge this wrong. Over time, they slowly become obsessed with this until it is the only thing they can think of. Bobby described it like "an itch that you just can't scratch". As such they act violently to try and avenge the wrong, however in the process, their obsession with revenge often leads to them harming innocent people. Sometimes their vengeance can spread on to others, who had nothing to do with them, but have or are doing something that is similar to their death.

It is possible that once they are avenged, they pass on, however often they are unable to cause this. Occasionally, they're people who are still alive, but on the verge of death.

[ИСТОРИЯ]
Specters are particularly vengeful ghosts that are less focused on personal vendetta, like most vengeful spirits do, and more on the concept of vengence. They are powerless as themselves, and can only act when possessing an individual. When they do so, they focus on this individual's repressed resentment and feeling of revenge, amplifying them to such degree the possessed individual is forced into violently following this resentment/revenge, which generally ends up in them murdering whomever they hold grudge for, be it the slightest.
